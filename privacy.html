Privacy & data

Privacy Policy

Candle Timer is designed to work locally on your Mac. The app does not collect personal data and does not make network connections.

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Applies to the Candle Timer macOS application.

At a glance: Candle Timer has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or app-to-server communication. It does not collect or transmit your personal information.

1. Who is responsible for the app

Candle Timer is an independent macOS application developed by Jonathan Nassim (“we”, “us” or “the developer”). For privacy questions, contact us using the support details on the Support page.

2. Information the app collects

The Candle Timer app does not collect personal data. It does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name or email address, does not collect device identifiers for analytics, and does not transmit app usage or diagnostic information to the developer.

The app does not connect to the internet. It does not retrieve market prices, connect to brokers or exchanges, or send information to third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

3. Preferences stored locally

To remember your choices, Candle Timer stores a small set of preferences in the macOS local preferences store (UserDefaults). These settings include:

  • Your selected candle timeframe.
  • Whether the countdown is shown in the menu bar.
  • Whether a sound plays when a candle closes.
  • Your selected macOS system alert sound.
  • Your clock adjustment value.

These preferences stay on your Mac. They are not sent to us or to another party by Candle Timer. The optional “Launch at login” setting is registered with macOS and managed through the system's Login Items controls.

4. Clock, sound and operating-system services

The countdown uses your Mac's system clock. The app itself does not contact a time server. macOS may synchronise the system clock separately according to your Mac's Date & Time settings.

If you enable a close chime, the app plays a macOS system sound locally. If you enable Launch at login, macOS manages the login item. These operating-system features do not mean that Candle Timer sends your personal data to the developer.

5. Third parties

The Candle Timer app does not share personal data with third parties because it does not collect or transmit personal data. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The app includes code derived from the open-source Kline Timer project under the MIT License; this code does not add network or data collection functionality. See Acknowledgements.

6. Data retention and deletion

The app does not hold a server-side copy of your information. The preferences described above remain in the local macOS preferences store until they are changed or removed on your Mac. Removing the app may not automatically remove every local preference record because macOS manages that store separately. We have no copy of those settings to retrieve or delete.

7. Website visits

This website is a static information site. It does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics tools or tracking pixels, and it does not provide a website account or contact form. The website's hosting provider may process basic technical request logs (such as IP address, request time and browser information) to deliver and protect the site. That processing is controlled by the hosting provider under its own terms and privacy practices.

8. Children's privacy

Candle Timer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are released. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated. The App Store privacy information will be kept consistent with the app's actual data practices.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the email address on our Support page.
