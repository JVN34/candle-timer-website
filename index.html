Simple by design

The next close, always close at hand.

Candle Timer stays in the macOS menu bar, giving you a clear countdown without another chart window or a trading account connection.

◷

Lives in your menu bar

Show the selected timeframe and countdown beside your other Mac menu bar items, or keep just the app icon visible.

24

24 built-in timeframes

From 15-second candles through minutes, hours, days and weeks, up to 12-month calendar candles.

10

Final 10-second signal

The countdown turns red and the menu bar item pulses during the final 10 seconds of each candle.

♪

Optional close chime

Turn on a short sound when a candle closes and choose from 14 built-in macOS system sounds.

±

Fine clock adjustment

Adjust the countdown by up to five seconds in 0.1-second steps to line it up with the clock shown by your chart.

⌘

Preferences stay on your Mac

Your timeframe, sound and display preferences are saved locally. Launch at login is optional and managed by macOS.

Choose your interval

From seconds to calendar months.

Select the candle length you want to follow. The chosen timeframe is remembered between launches.

✓Fixed-length candles align to UTC boundaries.
✓Weekly candles begin Monday at 00:00 UTC.
✓Monthly candles follow calendar months, including leap years.
✓The countdown is recalculated from the clock, so it re-syncs after sleep or a clock change.
