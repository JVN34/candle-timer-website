SUGGESTED APP REVIEW NOTES — CANDLE TIMER (macOS)

Candle Timer is a macOS menu bar countdown utility. It does not have a Dock icon
or a conventional main window. After launch, look for the Candle Timer status
item in the macOS menu bar (top-right area) and click it to open the panel.

The panel allows the reviewer to:
- Select one of 24 candle timeframes (15 seconds to 12 calendar months).
- View the remaining time to the selected candle close.
- See the countdown turn red during the final 10 seconds.
- Optionally enable a local macOS system-sound chime at candle close.
- Choose the system sound, adjust the clock offset and optionally enable
  Launch at login through macOS Login Items.

No account or sign-in is required. The app does not connect to the internet,
retrieve prices, access broker accounts, place trades, or collect personal data.
Preferences are stored locally on the Mac.

The countdown uses the Mac's system clock and UTC candle boundaries. The app
cannot verify or synchronise against a third-party chart's own clock.
