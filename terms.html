Important information about the purpose, timing behaviour and limitations of Candle Timer.

Effective date: 1 November 2026.

1. What Candle Timer is

Candle Timer is a menu bar countdown utility for macOS. It calculates the time remaining until the next boundary of a selected candle timeframe. It does not provide prices, charts, market data, trade recommendations or order execution.

2. Timekeeping and accuracy

The app calculates candle boundaries using your Mac's system clock and UTC-based intervals. Weekly candles begin on Monday at 00:00 UTC. Calendar-month intervals follow UTC calendar boundaries. The app does not connect to a time server or retrieve the clock used by any broker, charting service or exchange.

Countdown results can differ from another application because of differences in system time, chart time, market-session definitions, data-provider conventions, network latency or clock configuration. The optional clock adjustment is a manual convenience and does not verify or guarantee synchronisation with another service.

3. Not financial advice

Candle Timer is a timekeeping tool only. It is not a trading platform, broker, investment service or source of financial information. Nothing in the app or on this website is financial, investment, tax or trading advice. Do not rely on the countdown as the sole basis for a financial decision.

4. Availability and changes

The app is provided for use on compatible versions of macOS (macOS 13 or later at the time of publication). Features and compatibility may change in future versions. Keep macOS and the app up to date.
