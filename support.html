Frequently asked questions

What does Candle Timer do?

Candle Timer is a macOS menu bar utility. It counts down to the close of the current candle for the timeframe you select. It does not display prices, place trades or connect to a broker or exchange.

Which timeframes are available?

24 timeframes are included: 15s, 30s, 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 45m, 1h, 2h, 3h, 4h, 6h, 8h, 12h, 1D, 3D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M and 12M.

What clock does the countdown use?

The app uses your Mac's system clock and aligns candle boundaries to UTC. Make sure macOS is set to set the date and time automatically. The app does not connect to a time server itself.

Why might the countdown differ slightly from my chart?

Your chart may use a clock that differs slightly from your Mac's clock. Use Clock adjustment in the app to nudge the countdown by 0.1 seconds at a time, up to ±5 seconds. The app cannot read the chart's clock or verify its market-session settings.

Why does the countdown turn red?

The countdown turns red and the menu bar item pulses during the final 10 seconds of every candle. This behaviour is the same for every timeframe.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes. Sound on close is optional. When enabled, you can choose from 14 built-in macOS system sounds.

Does Candle Timer collect my data?

No. The app makes no network connections and collects no personal data. Your preferences are stored locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

Which macOS versions are supported?

Candle Timer requires macOS 13 or later.
